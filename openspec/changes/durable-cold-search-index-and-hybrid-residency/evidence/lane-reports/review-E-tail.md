### Verdict on bounds
- **Disk:** linear components (map, exceptions) bounded by construction; FTS5 shadows amortized-bounded (no unbounded-growth mechanism found — tombstones are reclaimed by `automerge`, `%_content`/`%_docsize` rows are hard-deleted), **but bytes/row are unproven for the production schema and the churn overshoot constant is unmeasured**. The "+120MB/1M" figure: unverifiable as stated.
- **WAL:** bounded per `ApplyBatch` (≤1 MiB wire batches); **not bounded** for the single-volume migration/rebuild — this is the one place where I would not sign off "bounded" without the P1-2 fix + measurement at 2M/5M.
- **RAM:** bounded (default ~2 MiB page cache/connection, ~192 KiB probe caches, streaming rebuild, 500 MiB budget untouched). Two unmeasured transient spots: candidate-query ephemeral materialization and FTS5 bulk-insert internals at 5M — both bounded by N but needing the probes above. `HostFoldSignature`: RAM-trivial, CPU fixed-bounded (≤~8.1M calls) and cached once per process, but unmeasured on the startup path.
- **Rebuild/reopen:** streaming peak (plus the P3 rowid vector on forget); leaves tombstone/merge garbage (P2-4); O(N) open cross-check (P3).
